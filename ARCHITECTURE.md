## 2. Core Astrodynamics Consensus Mechanics

### 2.1 The Two-Line Element (TLE) Challenge
Orbital debris tracking relies on Two-Line Element sets (TLEs) generated by radar surveillance networks (such as the US Space Surveillance Network). A TLE contains Keplerian orbital elements:
- Mean Motion ($n$, revolutions per day)
- Eccentricity ($e$)
- Inclination ($i$)
- Argument of Perigee ($\omega$)
- Right Ascension of Ascending Node ($\Omega$)

From mean motion and eccentricity, the semi-major axis $a$ and perigee altitude $h_p$ are derived:
$$a = \left( \frac{\mu}{(n \cdot \frac{2\pi}{86400})^2} \right)^{1/3}$$
$$h_p = a(1 - e) - R_\text{Earth}$$

Traditional EVM blockchains cannot fetch web feeds or compute transcendental orbital equations. **GenLayer Intelligent Contracts** leverage `gl.nondet.web.render` to fetch live TLE radar ephemeris directly from authenticated space surveillance networks and utilize `gl.vm.run_nondet` to compute decay vectors in consensus.

| `STATUS_UNRESOLVED` | HTTP timeout, 404, or security canary mismatch | **Escrow Protected** from premature liquidation |

## 4. Game-Theoretic Dispute Appeals

To eliminate single-oracle vulnerability and prevent corrupt adjudication:
1. When an AI verdict is rendered, the policy enters a **24-block (~72 seconds)** cooling-off window (`STATUS_AWAITING_PAYOUT`).
2. An appellant must stake a **10% dispute bond** (`dispute_bond = escrow * 10%`) and bind supplemental high-resolution radar logs (e.g., LeoLabs phased-array ephemeris).
3. The Supreme Astrodynamics Appellate Court evaluates the supplemental evidence:
   - **If Appeal Upheld:** The original verdict is reversed, bounty is disbursed according to corrected telemetry, and the 10% bond is returned to the appellant.
   - **If Appeal Dismissed:** The original verdict stands, and the 10% bond is forfeited directly to the opposing party.

## 5. Storage Layout Specification

The intelligent contract implements clean storage bindings on GenVM:
- `policies: TreeMap[str, DebrisRemovalPolicy]`: Primary policy database keyed by stringified policy IDs.
- `policy_ids: DynArray[str]`: Enumerable registry of all initiated orbital policies.
- `claimable_balances: TreeMap[str, bigint]`: Pull-payment safety buffers preventing stuck funds.
- `total_space_locked: bigint`: Aggregated GEN escrow locked in the protocol.
- `total_missions_settled: u32`: Counter of completed and verified deorbit missions.
